# SPEC-26 - NED University 🤖

My experience and projects from SPEC'26 Robotics Competition at NED University of Engineering & Technology.

## 🏆 Competitions Covered

### ⚔️ 1. Robo War
The ultimate battle of robots. Power, durability and driving skills.

Robots playing football - Autonomous & Manual categories.

Push the opponent out of the ring.
